## Build programmable money on Arc.

This hackathon is for teams building real financial applications on **Arc**, Circle's stablecoin-native L1, using **USDC** as core infrastructure for payments, settlement and value movement.

### Two tracks

Choose the track that best fits your project:

#### 1\. DeFi

Build stablecoin-native DeFi: lending, borrowing, swaps, liquidity, FX, yield, payments, treasury and fintech infrastructure. Strong projects should show why Arc's USDC-denominated fees, sub-second settlement and Circle integrations make the product better than a standard onchain app.

#### 2\. Agentic Economy

Build autonomous financial systems: AI agents that hold wallets, pay for services, settle jobs, rebalance funds, manage treasury or transact with other agents using USDC. Strong projects should show real agent autonomy, not just an AI wrapper.

### Core products to build with

Participants should build around the Arc and Circle product stack. These are the core resources we expect teams to use where relevant:

- **Arc** — Circle's stablecoin-native L1 for real-world financial applications, with USDC-denominated gas and sub-second settlement.
- **USDC** — the core money layer for payments, settlement, treasury, DeFi flows and agent transactions.
- **App Kits** — Circle SDKs for Send, Bridge, Swap and Unified Balance, helping teams build payment and liquidity workflows quickly.
- **Agent Stack** — starter kits and patterns for connecting AI agents to wallets, USDC payments and onchain actions.

### Who should apply

Teams who want to build a product and take it to market. Existing startups, projects and teams are welcome. The top teams will be offered places in an 8-week accelerator programme to support the journey from hackathon to launch.

## Challenge

### 🚀 Build on Arc — Overview

This is a 4-week online hackathon to build real products on **Arc**, Circle's stablecoin-native L1, using **USDC** as the core money layer for payments, settlement, treasury, DeFi and agent transactions.

### Two tracks to build in

- **DeFi** — stablecoin-native lending, borrowing, swaps, liquidity, FX, yield, payments, treasury and fintech infrastructure.
- **Agentic Economy** — autonomous AI agents that hold wallets and pay, settle and transact in USDC without a human in the loop.

### Core products

Projects should make meaningful use of the Arc and Circle stack where relevant:

- **Arc** — stablecoin-native L1 with USDC-denominated gas and sub-second settlement.
- **USDC** — payments, settlement, liquidity and treasury flows.
- **App Kits** — SDKs for Send, Bridge, Swap and Unified Balance.
- **Agent Stack** — starter kits and patterns for wallets, USDC payments and onchain actions in AI agents.

You can enter more than one track with the same project if it genuinely meets the requirements for each track.

### Key dates & what to submit

Submit at each checkpoint from your project page. Checkpoints 1 and 2 can be placeholders / works-in-progress. Only the final checkpoint must be complete.

- **Launch Event — Monday 13 July:** live kickoff.
- **Checkpoint 1 — Sunday 19 July:** create your project, add your team, and share your idea.
- **Checkpoint 2 — Sunday 26 July:** mid-submission — repository link and progress summary.
- **Registration closes — Saturday 8 August.**
- **Checkpoint 3 (Final Submission) — Sunday 9 August:** a functional MVP deployed on Arc, a public code repo, a 3-minute video pitch + demo, and a deck.
- **Demo Day — Thursday 20 August.**

_Submission deadlines are Anywhere on Earth (UTC-12). Submit early; the platform locks at the deadline and late final submissions cannot be judged._

### From hackathon to launch

The top teams, up to 8, win places in an 8-week accelerator programme with weekly workshops, 1-1 calls, and a cohort of builders alongside you.

### Resources

Start with the [Arc docs](https://docs.arc.io/), [App Kits](https://docs.arc.io/app-kit), [Circle developer platform](https://developers.circle.com/), [Agent Stack starter kits](https://github.com/circlefin/agent-stack-starter-kits), and the [Build on Circle Discord](https://discord.com/invite/buildoncircle).

### DeFi Track

## Build stablecoin-native DeFi on Arc.

Build lending, borrowing, swaps, liquidity, FX, yield, payments, treasury or fintech infrastructure using Arc and USDC.

### What we're looking for:

- Meaningful use of **Arc** and **USDC**
- Advanced programmable money flows such as conditional payments, onchain automation or multi-step settlement
- Payment, liquidity or treasury workflows using **App Kits** where relevant
- Applications that show why stablecoin-native infrastructure changes what is possible

**Core products:** Arc, USDC, App Kits, Circle Wallets, Circle Contracts, CCTP, Gateway, StableFX.

### Agentic Economy Track

## Build autonomous agents that transact on Arc.

Build AI agents that hold wallets, make payments, manage risk, settle jobs or transact with other agents using USDC.

### What we're looking for:

- Agents with clear decision logic tied to real signals
- Autonomous spending, payments or settlement flows using **USDC**
- Use of **Agent Stack** to connect agents to wallets, USDC payments and onchain actions
- Use of Nanopayments, Paymaster or App Kits where relevant for agent-to-agent or service payments

**Core products:** Arc, USDC, Agent Stack, App Kits, Circle Wallets, Circle Contracts, Nanopayments, Paymaster.
