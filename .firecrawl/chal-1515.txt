title = 🚀 Build on Arc — Overview
DESCRIPTION :: This is a 4-week online hackathon to build real products on Arc , Circle's stablecoin-native L1, using USDC as the core money layer for payments, settlement, treasury, DeFi and agent transactions. Two tracks to build in DeFi — stablecoin-native lending, borrowing, swaps, liquidity, FX, yield, payments, treasury and fintech infrastructure. Agentic Economy — autonomous AI agents that hold wallets and pay, settle and transact in USDC without a human in the loop. Core products Projects should make meaningful use of the Arc and Circle stack where relevant: Arc — stablecoin-native L1 with USDC-denominated gas and sub-second settlement. USDC — payments, settlement, liquidity and treasury flows. App Kits — SDKs for Send, Bridge, Swap and Unified Balance. Agent Stack — starter kits and patterns for wallets, USDC payments and onchain actions in AI agents. You can enter more than one track with the same project if it genuinely meets the requirements for each track. Key dates & what to submit Submit at each checkpoint from your project page. Checkpoints 1 and 2 can be placeholders / works-in-progress. Only the final checkpoint must be complete. Launch Event — Monday 13 July: live kickoff. Checkpoint 1 — Sunday 19 July: create your project, add your team, and share your idea. Checkpoint 2 — Sunday 26 July: mid-submission — repository link and progress summary. Registration closes — Saturday 8 August. Checkpoint 3 (Final Submission) — Sunday 9 August: a functional MVP deployed on Arc, a public code repo, a 3-minute video pitch + demo, and a deck. Demo Day — Thursday 20 August. Submission deadlines are Anywhere on Earth (UTC-12). Submit early; the platform locks at the deadline and late final submissions cannot be judged. From hackathon to launch The top teams, up to 8, win places in an 8-week accelerator programme with weekly workshops, 1-1 calls, and a cohort of builders alongside you. Resources Start with the Arc docs , App Kits , Circle developer platform , Agent Stack starter kits , and the Build on Circle Discord .
