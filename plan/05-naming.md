# Bailey — Naming Decision Record (2026-07-20)

**Chosen name: Bailey** — after George Bailey of *It's a Wonderful Life* (1946), cinema's most
beloved banker, whose Building & Loan always knew where every dollar lived ("the money's in Joe's
house… and the Kennedy house!"). The bank-run scene is the demo video's emotional frame: *a bank
that can account for every penny, instantly.*

Tagline: *"Your agents do the business. Bailey keeps the books."*

## Why it's safe (vetted 2026-07-20)

- The film lapsed into US **public domain** in 1974; Paramount's trademark covers only the film
  title; "George Bailey" is not trademarked; "Bailey" is an ordinary English surname (~100th most
  common in the US). The movie reference is a **defensible wink, not an IP claim** — keep it
  implicit in commercial copy (fine to speak in the hackathon video).
- No fintech, crypto, accounting, or AI-agent product uses bare "Bailey."
- Baileys Irish Cream is a different mark (BAILEYS, beverages class) — no software/financial
  registrations found.

## Clearance to-dos (before incorporation; none block the hackathon)

1. **USPTO TESS check** on application serial **98563320** ("Bailey Brand Management") — confirm
   it does not cover classes 36 (financial services) / 42 (SaaS).
2. Be aware of **b.well's "bailey™"** — a healthcare AI assistant launched Feb 2026. Different
   industry/class, but it's the nearest live AI product with the name; an examiner may surface it.
3. **Domains:** bailey.com is corporate-locked (since 1992, unobtainable) — register
   **bailey.xyz** and/or **getbailey.com / usebailey.com**; check bailey.ai availability.
4. Expect USPTO **surname-doctrine friction** (§2(e)(4)) on a bare word mark — plan for acquired
   distinctiveness or a compound/stylized mark at filing time.

## How the decision was made

Four naming rounds, ~70 candidates generated, 25 finalists individually web-vetted for collisions
(active companies, trademarks, npm/GitHub, domains) by adversarial research agents:

1. **General round** (heritage / agent-compounds / coined / on-chain): killed Botbank, Checksum,
   Summa (all taken), Fleetbooks, Blotter (risky). Survivors: Bursar 89, Proofbook 85, Arcary 83.
2. **Mythology & literature round**: killed Moneta (taken), Seshat (Seshat Bank exists — the
   perfect name was taken by almost exactly this product), Prospero (~10 financial entities);
   Thoth/Muninn/Boswell/Micawber risky. Survivor: Domesday 84 (doomsday homophone).
3. **Arc-rule check**: Circle's Brand Use Policy lists ARC as a Brand Asset and prohibits
   incorporating it into product names ("NO INCORPORATION", "NO REGISTRATION") — **Arcary and
   Arcount eliminated** despite 50+ Arc-named projects being tolerated in the hackathon itself
   (policy risk bites at the accelerator/incorporation stage, which is the prize).
4. **Movie round**: killed Moneypenny (Moneypenny Ltd UK), TARS (chatbot co + Solana token +
   Linux Foundation mark), Lucius (WB), Wolfe (Miramax licenses the character — the wink could
   never be spoken; predatory connotation), Clarence (crowded AI namespace), Tuppence (estate
   enforcement window + UK slang). Survivors: **Bailey 86**, Murray 81.

**Final leaderboard:** Bursar 89 · **Bailey 86 ← chosen** · Proofbook 85 · Domesday 84 · Murray 81
· Passbook (old working title) 62 — risky: passbook.ai is an active Indian fintech and Remitly
still held the "PASSBOOK BY REMITLY" mark (IC 036) per its Feb 2025 10-K.

The user chose Bailey over top-scoring Bursar, trading a slightly cleaner clearance path for the
strongest story: the judge's assessment was that Bailey has "better emotional material for a
3-minute video," with only routine clearance work (items 1–4 above) separating them.
